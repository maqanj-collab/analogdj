AnalogDJ for iPad · version 2026-10-28 09.21

AnalogDJ

Import your music folder (File → Import a folder) or drag files onto the window or a deck. AnalogDJ reads your tags, then works out BPM, beat grid, key and the RGB waveform for every track in the background. Hot cues, memory cues and playlists are saved automatically. The demo tracks shown before your first import are silent previews.

To use a controller, open Layout & sizes (the gear), press MIDI learn, click a control on screen, then move or press it on your controller.

On iPad: tap a track twice to load it, or touch and hold it (or a playlist) for more options. Tap a knob or fader twice to reset it. Drag knobs up and down, drag the jog to scratch or nudge, drag a waveform to scrub. Tracks you add are copied onto this iPad and stay available offline. Add AnalogDJ to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) to use it full screen.
